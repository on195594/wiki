## UX design for proactive agents — High signal

Image alt text from the source (the image itself is not archived):

> Agent action matrix: acting on something worth acting on is a useful action, not acting when nothing matters is useful silence, acting on what does not matter is noise, and not acting on what does is a missed opportunity

## UX design for proactive agents — Built for review / Where you already work

> Every check-in should be fast to review, because review is how your feedback gets back into memory.

> Similarly, Robin leads with a one- or two-sentence headline and puts the detail in a thread reply, so you can tell at a glance whether something needs you.
