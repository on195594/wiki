## Captured source outline
- Nuanced tried to give plans a persistent home and carry intent through specification, implementation, review and verification. The author wanted developers to retain a coherent mental model as agents generated code faster than people could inspect it.
- The retrospective names four causes of failure: conflating planning with a plan; improving model capability reducing detailed upfront instruction; long AI-generated specs that users did not want to read (the Spec Tour added another text layer); and a linear separation of planning from building that made returning to earlier reasoning awkward.
- The author contrasts `plan → approve → execute` with `understand → act → inspect → clarify → adjust → act again`. Planning remains inside the loop rather than necessarily being a large document.
- The unresolved question is how humans keep their understanding current when many agents change a system concurrently, and where human attention is most useful.
